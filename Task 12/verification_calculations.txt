VERIFICATION CALCULATIONS

1) 16-bit Wh capacity
   2^16 - 1 = 65535 Wh
   = 65.535 kWh

2) Example days at 10 kWh/day
   65.535 / 10 = 6.5535 days

3) 15-minute readings per year
   24 × 4 × 365 = 35,040

4) Worst-case annual sum based on 16-bit maximum interval
   35,040 × 65,535 = 2,296,346,400 Wh

5) Signed 32-bit maximum
   2,147,483,647
   Therefore 2,296,346,400 > 2,147,483,647

6) District reading count
   800,000 × 35,040 = 28,032,000,000 readings/year
