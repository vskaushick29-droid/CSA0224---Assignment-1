TARIFF REVISION SOP

1. Identify the approved tariff change.
2. Convert all monetary rates to paise.
3. Edit the single tariff-constant section in the source.
4. Do not modify billing formulas in multiple locations.
5. Compile using:
   gcc -std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -pedantic -O2 -o task11_final_design task11_final_design.c
6. Confirm zero compiler warnings.
7. Execute the boundary suite:
   0, 100, 150, 200, 350 units.
8. Confirm rollover:
   65400 -> 120 = 256 units.
9. Confirm district-scale accumulator remains within long long.
10. Record the test output.
11. Only after validation, distribute the updated executable.
