At Rs 950.00 per bill, about 22605 bills reach INT_MAX paise.

District-scale 800,000-bill result:
  long long paise = 76000000000
  exceeds INT_MAX? YES
  exceeds LONG_MAX? NO

Double vs long long:
double stores approximately 15 decimal digits; long long stores exact integer paise.
For this district total, double can represent the integer magnitude exactly,
but long long is preferred for billing because paise arithmetic is exact by construction.
